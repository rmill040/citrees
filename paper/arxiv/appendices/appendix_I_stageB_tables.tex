\section{Stage~B construction: cost and real-data tables}
\label{app:stageB-tables}

\Cref{tab:stageB-scaling,tab:stageB-fixed-budget,tab:stageB-real,tab:stageB-power} give the fitting-time, fixed-budget control, real-data, and nominal-level split-rate
results for the Stage~B construction comparison summarized in
\cref{sec:results-stageB}; the design is in \cref{sec:experiments-stageB} and the size-matched comparison is \cref{tab:stageB-only} in the main text. The entries of \cref{tab:stageB-scaling,tab:stageB-real} are medians over five seeds; \cref{tab:stageB-fixed-budget} is a single pass with 500 null and 200 alternative replicates per cell; \cref{tab:stageB-power} pools 2,500 replicates per cell, five seeds of 500, and reports rates, not medians.
