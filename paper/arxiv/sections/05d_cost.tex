\subsection{What the corrections cost}
\label{sec:results-practical}

The two corrections set the permutation budget of every Stage~A test and every
per-threshold Stage~B test, and \cref{tab:runtime-ablations} measures what that
costs by changing one setting at a time; time ratios above 1 are slower.
Removing both corrections cuts
forest time to 0.03 to 0.34 of the reference, but it changes the statistical
rule as well. It deepens single trees from a mean of 6.5 and 7.2 levels to 10.7
and 13.4 in classification and regression and moves CIT top-10 recovery by up
to 0.063, while the CIF downstream score moves by at most 0.004. The selected configuration keeps both corrections.
\Cref{app:complexity-details} gives the work terms each change affects.

Adaptive stopping recovers none of this cost at the minimum budget, where it is
inert by construction (\cref{sec:theory-adaptive}). Switching it off leaves
every tree unchanged, so the ratios near 1 measure only the cost of the check,
and a forest run on one worker with the full thread pool inside each tree gives
the same result (0.95 to 1.01). At the auto budget, where the rule can act, adaptive stopping
brings the tree to 0.14 to 0.37 and the forest to 0.11 to 0.25 of their time
with no stopping, with recovery changes of at most 0.030.

Of the runtime controls, the threshold scan has the largest effect: testing
candidates in order of weighted child impurity and stopping at the first
rejection saves the most, since the threshold correction gives each of the $K$
candidates a budget that grows with $K$. The candidate count comes next. For the forest,
exact threshold search is 1.3 to 19 times slower than the 256-bin histogram,
and a 32-bin histogram is 7 to 21 times faster, with downstream changes of at
most 0.009. Feature scanning, the Stage~A
counterpart of the threshold scan, matters most on the real regression
datasets. The pattern holds on the 14 Benchmark-size datasets, with up to 20,000
observations and 5,000 features (\cref{app:scaling-curves}). The slowest single
tree is gisette at 29 minutes, where the feature correction over 5,000 features
sets a large minimum budget for each feature test.
